# Decisions needed before the next P0 phase

1. **Staging access and safeguards:** Can we use a separate Supabase/Firebase staging project, with test accounts, a verified backup and explicit approval for non-production migrations? Safe default: no live database changes until confirmed.
2. **Identity provisioning:** Should Firebase remain the authentication provider while each verified subject maps one-to-one to an existing Supabase `auth.users.id`? Safe default: yes, only after an audited provisioning and recovery design; never infer mappings from email or create them implicitly. Decide how existing accounts are matched and handle unmapped users.
3. **Experimental feed:** Is the current opt-in client status check and its polling/fallback behavior acceptable? The check is fail-closed for cooperating clients, but offline and older clients cannot be remotely stopped. Safe default: leave ordinary feed and reactions alone, keep the experiment off, and do not market this as an absolute kill switch.
4. **Reaction shadow design:** May we build an isolated, explicitly non-mutating preview on staging and define snapshot/compare semantics? Calling the current toggle RPC after the ordinary write would toggle it again. Safe default: do not call the mutating RPC in shadow. Before replacement require verified mappings for all active staging users, independent concurrent-writer tests, and seven days of meaningful shadow comparisons with zero unexplained divergences; define coverage, denominator and concurrency handling first.
5. **Stake, slashing and corpus:** Should these remain disabled? Safe default: no staking, slashing, automatic penalties or corpus API until explicitly approved and reviewed; no commitment to a launch date.
6. **Data rights and account deletion:** What data must be exported, retained or deleted across Supabase, Firebase, Firestore and browser storage, and what legal/retention rules apply? Safe default: no destructive deletion, no claim that partial exports are complete, and obtain privacy/legal advice where needed.

7. **Reaction tier schema:** What values and semantics should an actual reaction tier have? Migration 0007 adds `reaction_tier_source` to reactions, not a tier value. Safe default: keep tier/stake features disabled and do not invent or backfill tier values until the schema and product behavior are reviewed.
8. **Reaction concurrency:** Should reactions gain a reliable monotonic version or managed `updated_at`, and how will deletions and recreation be tracked? Safe default: keep the preview token null and do not use it for concurrency or shadow safety; require a reviewed migration, reliable version semantics and staging tests first.

Local tests and staging checks do not by themselves establish production or legal readiness. No credentials are needed in chat; use approved configuration channels when staging is authorized.
