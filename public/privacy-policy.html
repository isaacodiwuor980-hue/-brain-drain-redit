# Privacy Policy _Last updated: 2026-10-08_ Brain Drain ("we", "us", "our") operates the Brain Drain application ("the app"). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it. ## Data We Collect ### Data you provide - **Account data:** display name, bio, avatar (if you sign in with Google) - **Content you create:** posts, comments, brains (decision trees), missions, stories, messages - **Profile settings:** privacy toggles, notification preferences ### Data collected automatically - **Usage analytics:** page views, button clicks, session start/end, feature usage — used to improve the app. No personally identifying content in payloads. - **Error reports:** if the app crashes, an error report (message, stack trace, route) is sent to our server to help us fix bugs. - **Device info:** browser type, approximate location (country-level via IP), connection type — used for performance optimization. ### Data we do NOT collect - We do not read your private messages content for advertising - We do not sell your data to third parties - We do not use your data to train AI models without explicit consent ## How We Use Your Data - To provide the app's features (creating, displaying, and syncing your content) - To authenticate you and keep your account secure - To diagnose and fix bugs (via error reports) - To understand how features are used (via anonymized analytics) - To comply with legal obligations ## Data Storage - **Local storage:** most data is stored in your browser's IndexedDB. This data stays on your device unless you sign in and sync. - **Cloud storage:** if you sign in, your data syncs to Supabase (Postgres) and Firebase (Firestore) for cross-device access. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. - **Retention:** we retain your data for as long as your account is active. Deleted content is removed within 30 days. ## Your Rights You have the right to: - **Access** a copy of your data (Settings → Advanced → Export Account Data) - **Delete** your data (Settings → Advanced → Delete Account) - **Object** to processing for specific purposes - **Withdraw consent** for analytics (Settings → Privacy → Analytics & Telemetry) To exercise these rights, email privacy@braindrain.app. ## Third-Party Services - **Firebase Auth** (Google) — authentication - **Supabase** — database hosting - **Google Fonts** — font delivery Each service has its own privacy policy. We only share the minimum data necessary to use these services. ## Children's Privacy The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it. ## Changes to This Policy We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice in the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance. ## Contact Email: privacy@braindrain.app --- *This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), and the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019).*
